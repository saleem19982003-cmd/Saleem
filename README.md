## 🌟 About Saleem

**Saleem** is designed to address the primary integration challenges faced by refugees and displaced individuals from African nations living in Egypt:
1. **Communication & Dialect Barriers** (Egyptian Colloquial Arabic vs. Modern Standard Arabic).
2. **Awareness of Legal Rights & UNHCR Procedures**.
3. **Access to Healthcare, Education, and Support Services**.
4. **Employment & Digital Skill Development**.

---

## 🚀 Key Features & 10 Core Modules

1. **Authentication & User Management**: Secure session management, EncryptedSharedPreferences, biometrics, and nationality-to-language onboarding.
2. **Egyptian & African Dialect Translator**: Real-time text translation, Web Speech multi-accent voice recognition, and contextual slang dictionary (*Malish*, *Khalaas*, *Yalla*, *Selam*, *Jambo*, *Sannu*).
3. **Multilingual AI Assistant**: Context-aware AI assistant answering housing, transit, banking, and UNHCR residency questions in 9+ languages.
4. **Interactive Culture Guide**: Lessons & interactive quizzes on Egyptian customs, etiquette, public transit rules, and market bargaining.
5. **Legal Support & Refugee Rights**: Step-by-step procedure guides, legal rights documentation, interactive residency checklists, and emergency hotlines.
6. **Services Directory & Maps**: Geolocated directory for clinics, language schools, legal aid desks, and NGO offices.
7. **Community Hub**: Peer Q&A forum, verified volunteer mentors, and cultural exchange meetups.
8. **Learning Hub - Programming Education**: Free courses in Web & Android development, portfolio certificates, and remote tech job board.
9. **Awareness & Inclusion**: Anti-discrimination campaigns and confidential incident reporting with tracking codes.
10. **Profile & Settings**: Account management, biometric security toggles, and official contact channels.

## 🌍 Supported Languages

- **East & Horn of Africa**: Amharic (አማርኛ), Somali (Soomaali), Tigrinya (ትግርኛ), Swahili (Kiswahili), Oromo (Afaan Oromoo).
- **North Africa & Middle East**: Egyptian Colloquial Arabic (العامية المصرية), Sudanese Arabic, Modern Standard Arabic, French.
- **West, Central & Southern Africa**: Hausa, Yoruba, Igbo, Wolof, Lingala, Zulu.
- **International**: English.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Architecture

### Android Native App
- **Language**: Kotlin 1.9
- **UI Framework**: Jetpack Compose + Material Design 3
- **Architecture**: MVVM + Clean Architecture
- **Dependency Injection**: Hilt 2.50
- **Local Persistence**: Room Database 2.6
- **Security**: EncryptedSharedPreferences & DataStore

### Web Application & Simulator
- **Frontend**: HTML5, Vanilla CSS3 (Glassmorphic Design), JavaScript (ES6+)
- **Speech API**: Web Speech API (`webkitSpeechRecognition`)
- **Translation & AI**: Live MyMemory API & Open AI Integration
